Blackjack Duels! is a turn-based, local multiplayer game that reimagines classic Blackjack with a dark twist: Death himself takes center stage as the dealer. Players must build a hand as close to 21 as possible without going over, pitting their card-playing prowess against one another to secure the ultimate prize — their lives. Outlast your rivals and rack up high scores to claim your seat at the top of the leaderboard.

Developed as a rapid prototype for a Data Structures course, this game serves as an interactive demonstration of fundamental C++ containers. Built under tight time constraints, the project uses <code class="font-mono font-medium text-sm py-0.5 px-1 rounded-md bg-accent inline-block">std::vector</code>, <code class="font-mono font-medium text-sm py-0.5 px-1 rounded-md bg-accent inline-block">std::list</code>, and <code class="font-mono font-medium text-sm py-0.5 px-1 rounded-md bg-accent inline-block">std::deque</code> to model card management, player instances, and health tracking. I was responsible for designing the core game loop and state architecture to manage the card mechanics, player health, and turn-order systems.
