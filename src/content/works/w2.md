Perx is a coupon collection application design to replace disposable printed coupons with accessible digital coupons. It connects consumers with merchants by allowing them to easily browse, purchase, and redeem digital discounts in participating physical stores via QR codes. Perx integrates a rewards system where users accumulate Loyalty points through completed transactions and successful referrals to encourage long-term customer engagement and brand loyalty for merchants.

This system is built as a group project for my Software Engineering courses. I spearheaded the end-to-end full-stack development, which includes implementing intuitive client interfaces for both consumer and merchant subsystems, developing the backend services for coupon management and automated loyalty points system tracking, and integrating the payment processing via the PayPal API.
