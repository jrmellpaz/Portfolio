Shelfwise is a demand forecasting tool that helps small businesses figure out what their shelves need next. Upload sales history, generate forecasts for any product, and get restocking decisions backed by real numbers, without any spreadsheet or data science background needed.

I built it as my undergraduate thesis. It classifies each product's demand pattern, runs several forecasting models, and automatically picks the best fit, so steady sellers, seasonal spikes, and slow-moving items all get handled differently. A built-in advisor, powered by a large-language model, then turns each forecast into plain-language advice on what to do next.
