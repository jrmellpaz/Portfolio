The Alumni Registration System is an end-to-end web application designed to replace the manual, paper-based onboarding with a seamless digital experience. The public-facing registration form guides applicants through entering structured data and submitting membership fee proof of payment, streamlining the process for new applicants. Admins can efficiently manage the ever-growing applicant and member database. The two-step verification process allows them to verify academic credentials and cross-reference payment receipts.

Developed as a group project for my Project Management course, the platform was built for the UP Alumni Association – Cebu Chapter. I primarily handled the frontend and backend development of the registration form and the admin-facing pages, focusing on creating an intuitive user journey. By ensuring the interfaces were accessible and tailored specifically to the needs of both alumni applicants and administrative staff, I helped deliver a frictionless workflow from initial application to final approval.
